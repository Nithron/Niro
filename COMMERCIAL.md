# Niro — Commercial & Binary Licensing

This document explains how companies, MSPs, and resellers can use Niro commercially and how Official Build Artifacts (container images, packages, update repos) and how the official hosted Niro bot fits in are licensed.

> **Summary**
> - Source code: available under the Nithron Community License (NCL) for **non-commercial** use and contributions.
> - Commercial use, hosting, resale, or binary redistribution requires a **Commercial License**.
> - Official Build Artifacts are licensed under a **Binary EULA** and may not be mirrored or resold.

## Ways to Use Niro

### 1) Personal / Non-Commercial (Free)
- Read/modify source, compile locally for personal lab use.
- Contribute PRs under NCL.
- No redistribution of binaries or hosted service offerings.

### 2) Business Internal Use (Commercial License)
- Use Official Build Artifacts or self-built binaries **inside your company**.
- Includes updates via Nithron’s signed repository.
- No redistribution or hosting for third parties.

### 3) Service Provider / Reseller (Commercial + Addendum)
- Host Niro for clients, manage deployments, or bundle it in your product.
- Requires an MSP/Reseller addendum (per-instance pricing or revenue share).

## Hosted Niro (the official bot)
The official Niro bot, hosted by Nithron, is a service and not covered by the NCL. Anyone can add it to their Discord server.
- Free tier: the core features at no cost.
- Premium: paid through Discord (in-app subscriptions) or directly through Nithron (Stripe).
- Use of the hosted service is governed by the Niro Terms of Service and Privacy Policy (to be published before public launch).

## License Channels

### A) Patreon Subscription (Individuals/Indie)
Active Patreon subscribers get:
- Access to Official Build Artifacts (stable/nightly per tier).
- Binary EULA granting use on a limited number of self-hosted instances (per tier).
- Priority community support.
> Companies should use a commercial agreement instead of Patreon.

### B) Commercial Agreement (Company/MSP)
Email **licensing@nithron.com** with:
- Company name, use case (internal/MSP), expected instance count, support needs.

We offer:
- Per-instance or annual subscription (updates + support SLAs).
- Reseller/MSP rights (hosted service, managed deployments).
- Optional white-label branding rights.
